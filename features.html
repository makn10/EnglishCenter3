Contextual Chat

Module 02

Live Expert Classes

Technology is great, but human connection is vital for mastering a language. Our live sessions are hosted via premium video infrastructure, ensuring you get real-time mentorship from globally certified instructors.

  • Strictly Small Batches

    We cap our live classes at 15 students max. This guarantees that every student gets a chance to speak and interact directly with the teacher.

  • Breakout Rooms

    During the class, the teacher will split you into pairs in private digital rooms to practice debates, dialogues, and discussions.

  • Cloud Recordings

    Missed a class? No problem. Full HD recordings of your live classes are instantly uploaded to your dashboard and saved for 6 months.

CEFR Tracker

Vocab Bank

Fluency Score

Weekly Reports
